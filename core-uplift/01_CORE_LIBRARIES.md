## 0. How it was checked, and the measurements

- Dead code: every `M.*`, `V.*`, `History.*` and `L["..."]` defined in the four folders was grepped across all
  addon folders (including `PourSocialScore_GUI`) and `tools/` (harness). Local functions and top-level locals were
  checked per file.
- Resident cost: `lua5.1` with `tools/harness/wow_stub.lua`, `collectgarbage("count")` around each file
  (TOC order), and around line ranges compiled on their own (`luac5.1 -s` for compiled size). Scripts in the
  scratchpad (`review/tools/measure.lua`, `ranges.lua`, `bench_*.lua`). Lua 5.1 on x86-64; the game's numbers
  differ in absolute terms, the ratios hold.

Per-file resident cost after load (KB): Localization 35.5, Globals 34.1, Loader 4.9, SavedData 15.7, Perf 23.4,
Events 2.1, Whois 20.0, Communities 24.0, MainlineCompat 0.0, ChatHistory 101.5, ChatBlock 59.9, ChatFilters 66.6,
PlayerIgnoreList 81.5, PlayerQuery 12.8, GuildIgnoreList 143.9, WhoisHarvest 19.7, Upgrade 30.1,
PourSocialScore.lua 16.3, Options 22.9, BlizzardUI 20.0. Core total 735 KB (stub frames included).
Libraries total 347 KB (ImportExport 43.0, PlayerView 54.4, GuildView 51.4, EventsView 33.2, Commands 27.9).

Benchmarks (Lua 5.1, this machine):
- A `PourSocialScoreDB.<key>` read through the SavedData router: 0.127 us; a direct table read: 0.008 us (16x).
  The 7 option reads at the top of `M.PSS_ChatFilters_Evaluate`: 1.15 us per chat line.
- `History.Clear` walking a full keep (2000 packed lines): 2.9 ms and about 1.6 MB of garbage per call.
- Two `canonGuild` calls (guild source `decide`, per line from a guild member): 1.2 us.
